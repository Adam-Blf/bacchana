## Direction contract

THESIS: Bacchana is the loto de la salle des fêtes, not a tavern poster. Every game is a carton de loto of its own coloured card stock, every choice drops a translucent red jeton, every prompt is a boule drawn and called aloud. It refuses the category default of a dark neon party app and the incumbent flat purple print.

OWN-WORLD: deep plum night table as ground in dark, pale formica mint in light; four saturated card-stock cartons (jaune poussin, rose bonbon, bleu lagon, vert anis) with fixed dark ink; one jeton red reserved for what can be pressed or is chosen; logo purple as the boulier frame. Die-cut rounded cartons that sit on the table with a real short shadow, ruled number cells, circular boules and jetons, Rubik Black numerals and titles over Chivo text.

STORY: the table understands at once which carton to take, sees the red jeton where the action is, and trusts the caller's boule to say whose turn it is.

FIRST VIEWPORT: welcome on a 390 phone. The name BACCHANA set as seven drawn boules across the top, the tagline under it, then one large carton holding la tablée: each player row starts with a numbered boule, the input is a ruled cell, and the full-width red jeton button "Pousser la porte" closes the carton.
